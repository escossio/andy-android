Andy Presence — original editorial bust, 2026-10

Original character geometry, materials, transform rig and animation data created
specifically for escossio/andy-android. The design uses a sculpted continuous
face, a static asymmetric swept bob, champagne accents and a tailored graphite
jacket with Jade and Plum clothing insets. It is an adult stylized fictional
character, not a likeness or scan of a real person.

All shapes were authored from original surface profiles, curved patches and
mathematical deformations. No third-party character, mesh, texture, scan,
photograph, copyrighted character reference or real-person data was used.
No image texture or external resource is required or referenced by andy.glb.
The static hair mesh is part of the character; there is no hair simulation.

The self-contained glTF 2.0 binary includes named Torso, Head, Jaw, Eyes, Gaze,
WaveArm, Jade and Plum transform nodes. This is a rigid stylized rig, not a
skinned or scanned human. Four local clips document Rest, Attentive, Blink
and Greeting. The app continues to drive these nodes with deterministic
visual values; clips do not introduce an independent animation clock.
